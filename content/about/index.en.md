# About CodeLab
CodeLab is a personal blog focused on backend development and AI Agent practice. It shares technical knowledge, hands-on projects, and the experience and reflections I have gathered through my work.

By documenting, reviewing, and organizing these experiences, I hope to turn scattered practice into reusable methods and learn with others who are interested in backend development and AI Agents.

# Author
Hi, I’m 程序员羊肉, a backend developer and a Xiaohongshu creator. I started by sharing my learning experiences on Xiaohongshu and have since built an audience of more than 2,500 followers.

CodeLab began as a way for me to build a personal blog: to record what I learn and practice, and gradually distill it into a methodology of my own. I hope these notes can help more people.

You can also treat CodeLab as an open knowledge community. If you would like to share your own experience, contributions and pull requests are welcome.
